# Identify the subject without a proposed binding

## Purpose

I will test whether Jev can identify a claim’s subject from the note before seeing a proposed binding. The app will compare the extracted service with that proposal, without changing the service used for the numerical verdict.

This follows the separate-call comparison, which improved clean displays but still misclassified subjects and withheld correct guidance. The new task asks for a service name rather than matched, conflict or unresolved.

## Frozen comparison

The diagnostic reuses 27 controlled notes on nine inspected recordings: plain, negated and boundary wording, with six confirmed claims per note. Train Ticket supplies 68 observed service names; Online Boutique supplies 12. The complete inventories come from inputs, not subject annotations.

Each of three rounds includes one direct subject call per note, two fresh proposal checks (clean and deliberately swapped) and two unchanged numerical verdict calls. The budget is 405 calls and 2,430 answers. All verdict calls run regardless of subject results.

The direct request contains the full note and observed service names. Its six questions ask Jev to select the unique subject, or unresolved. It contains no proposed binding, measurements or reference answers. The proposal-check and numerical verdict bodies remain exact controls from the earlier comparison.

## Display and assessment

The app displays a verdict only when both selected answers reach 0.70 and the extracted service equals the supplied service. Unresolved, missing answers and sentence quarantines withhold guidance. The app retains the supplied verdict binding even when the extracted subject differs.

One direct reply supports both clean and swapped comparisons. Unique subject accuracy and actual costs count that reply once. Original-verdict accuracy, safe displays, unsafe displays, withholding and paired gains and losses retain full denominators by application, wording and round. A display under a swapped subject counts as unsafe even when the verdict coincides.

The candidate must identify every subject correctly, preserve all correct clean baseline displays with at least 90% coverage, and display no unsafe swapped guidance in every panel and round. The fresh proposal check is a comparator, not a replacement candidate.

## Limits

The same assistant wrote and inspected these notes and references. Human and independent reviews remain zero. Every tested subject uniquely resolves; authentic reports and genuinely unresolved subjects remain untested. The direct task changes instructions and answer options as well as removing proposals, so a result cannot establish proposal anchoring as the cause. Shared replies are not independent observations. No protected recordings open.
