What stayed fixed?

The ledger, numerical eligibility rules, verdict definitions, Jev model and 0.70 display boundary stay unchanged. Each request contains three independent questions about the same proposition; all three share its reference. The earlier one-of-each-class pattern disappears. Wording roles and typed references stay out of requests.

Supported means the evidence establishes the statement; contradicted means it establishes its opposite; unanswerable means neither follows. Null mapped trace counts stay unknown. An eligible signed change of zero is an observed number. Counts and changes alone do not establish health or cause. Passing this diagnostic opens no protected data and does not establish real-report performance.
