Read the numerical policy

Metrics require both missing fractions ≤20% and absolute scaled change ≥3. Duration requires at least five spans per window, positive numeric starting duration, numeric ending duration, and absolute relative change ≥25%. Negative changes count. Quiet requires eligible observations below the boundary; unknown means no eligible observation. Any tied strongest eligible metric is accepted.

Trace coverage: at least five spans in each window is adequate, no spans in either window or no mapped trace is absent, and other counts are limited. These are research rules, not significance tests or health assessments.
