What does this confirmation test?

Bound text asks six questions with both service ledgers. Scoped facts asks the same questions separately, with one ledger per call. Both retain the full note. Grouping and scope change together here; their isolated diagnostic remains available in Study 14.

The literal controlled wording and selection rules were committed before new telemetry access. Claim spans and service names are supplied by preparation. Automatic claim extraction and service attribution are not evaluated. These are authored controls, not independently authored analyst reports.

Nine fresh recordings consume the remaining RE3 reserves. The earlier 22-case cause-evaluation panel, RE3 Sock Shop and 140 RE1 reserves remain unopened. The model, numerical policy and uncalibrated 0.70 display boundary stay fixed.
