What counts as an answer?

Supported: eligible observations establish the statement. Contradicted: eligible observations establish its opposite. Unanswerable: neither follows. An ineligible measurement cannot establish a threshold or direction claim. A missing trace mapping supplies no count; an explicitly recorded zero supplies a count. Changes alone cannot establish health or causality.

Each card contains one reference of each class, with positions shuffled. The model does not receive that balance. Fixed templates and matched opposite statements can create shortcuts; results do not establish performance on authentic reports. The typed reference evaluator is correct by construction and does not parse unrestricted text. Earlier ML predicts a different target and is unscored here.
