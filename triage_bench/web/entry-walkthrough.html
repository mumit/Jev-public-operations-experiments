Policy and field guide

Service and metric: choose one observed service and one of its catalog channels. CPU means cpu; memory means mem.

Comparison: material magnitude uses absolute scaled change of at least 3.0. Positive direction uses signed scaled change greater than zero.

Assertion: select the stated condition for a claim or yes/no check. Select its negation for “not material,” below 3.0, zero or negative, as appropriate to the comparison.

Window: this workflow supports before-incident versus after-incident observations. Ask the source when wording leaves a field unclear.
