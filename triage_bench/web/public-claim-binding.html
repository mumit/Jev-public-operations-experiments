What changes at each step?

Numbered lookup exactly repeats the earlier six-question report request. Bound text puts each claim’s ID, exact sentence and service in the question while preserving the entire state. One claim keeps that question and both ledgers but removes the other questions. Scoped facts keeps the one-claim question and complete report, removing only the other service’s ledger.

Compare adjacent steps to separate binding, question grouping and evidence scope. Claim bindings come from stored structured metadata; an unrestricted text extractor is not being tested. Numerical rules and the uncalibrated 0.70 boundary stay unchanged. Earlier responses stay separate, and protected data stays unopened.
