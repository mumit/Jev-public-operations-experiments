What does this comparison test?

Both presentations receive exactly the same two-service fact ledger and six sentences. Atomic questions include their sentence directly. Report questions locate a numbered sentence in the shared operations note. Every sentence explicitly names its service. The numerical and verdict rules stay fixed.

Supported means eligible evidence establishes the claim; contradicted means it establishes its opposite; unanswerable means neither follows. Missing data stays unknown. Authored reports on inspected application faults do not establish real-report understanding or telecom readiness. Protected data stays unopened.
