Read the frozen success criteria and task limits

Jev needs at least 90% role, service, meaning, accepted-binding and end-to-end agreement in every application and round, plus 90% whole-note correctness, complete calls, zero accepted nonclaims or review-only candidates, zero unsafe displays and correct display of at least half the supported claims.

Nine atomic assertions occur per note: six resolvable single claims, one ambiguous claim and two assertions in one compound. Compound and ambiguous sentences go to review. This pipeline does not split compounds or extract arbitrary spans. Code supplies sentence boundaries. The notes and annotations come from the experiment author, not independent analysts. Measurements are already inspected; this tests the bounded task, not generalization to authentic reports.

Role-only and meaning-only are diagnostics. Combined must also lose no role, service, meaning, accepted-correct or end-to-end count against the fresh baseline, and accept no wrong bindings. Six independent typed questions share the whole note without measurements. Code combines their replies at an uncalibrated 0.70 probability boundary. The second request uses the accepted subject bindings and their service ledgers. A correct verdict on the wrong binding counts as a failure.
